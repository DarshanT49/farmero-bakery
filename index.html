Our Journey

Born in a Bengaluru Kitchen, Perfected for Your Table

We started with a single cast-iron Dutch oven and a stubborn obsession: bringing honest, zero-chemical European sourdough to Bengaluru.

Traditional bakeries often rush dough with artificial bread improvers and commercial dry yeast. At The Flour Atelier, we chose the ancient, slow way. Our mother culture—affectionately named "Kaveri"—has been fed twice daily since 2018 in Indiranagar.

Today, our bakery serves morning walkers, creative studio teams from Domlur, and families celebrating milestone birthdays across Bangalore.
